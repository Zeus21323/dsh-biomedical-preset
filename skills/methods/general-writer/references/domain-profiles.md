# Domain Profiles

Select one primary profile after classifying the study. Apply secondary profiles only when the work is genuinely interdisciplinary.

## Generic

- Match section structure to the study type and venue rather than forcing IMRaD everywhere.
- Require source-to-claim traceability, calibrated language, reproducible objects, and explicit limitations.
- Ask only about unresolved choices that materially change the manuscript.

## Computer Science

Use for AI, ML, systems, software engineering, security, networking, HCI, and computing methods.

- Record datasets, tasks, baselines, metric direction, hardware, software versions, tuning budgets, seeds/runs, uncertainty, and evaluation protocol.
- Separate performance, efficiency, robustness, usability, privacy, fairness, and security claims.
- Restrict `outperforms` to evaluated baselines and `state of the art` to a justified contemporaneous benchmark set.
- Include ablations, sensitivity, failure cases, and threats to validity when supplied or required by the approved plan.
- Keep method/system description, evaluation result, and interpretation distinct.

## Bioinformatics And Biomedical Computation

Use for omics, clinical-bioinformatics, survival, enrichment, immune infiltration, and single-cell studies.

- Audit sample metadata, contrasts, inclusion/exclusion, batch handling, statistical tests, multiple-testing correction, validation cohorts, and software/database versions.
- Map one major Results question to one Results subsection and one major figure when the evidence supports that organization.
- Group multi-panel figures by biological claim, not chart type; record panel inputs, tests, corrections, and messages.
- Report gene identifiers, species/build, assay/platform, preprocessing, thresholds, cohort sizes, censoring, and external validation where applicable.
- Use literature to interpret findings, never to manufacture discoveries missing from result artifacts.
- Require embedded major figures and complete legends for DOCX delivery.

## Empirical Economics And Social Science

Use for panel, cross-sectional, policy, DID, IV, RDD, causal-inference, and regression-based papers.

- Define sample unit, outcome Y, core explanatory/treatment X, controls, mediators, heterogeneity variables, and robustness alternatives with canonical symbols.
- Preserve raw data, normalize merge keys, and reconcile sample flow arithmetically.
- Match model, fixed effects, clustering, treatment assignment, and identification assumptions to the design.
- Resolve every regression claim to an exact table and column; never report prose-only coefficients.
- Distinguish association from causality, statistical significance from economic magnitude, and percent from percentage points.
- Keep baseline, heterogeneity, robustness, mechanism, and endogeneity specifications in clearly scoped tables.
- Treat abnormal signs, zeros, standard errors, fit statistics, or sample changes as diagnostics before interpretation.

## Review And Evidence Synthesis

- Define databases/sources, search dates, queries, inclusion/exclusion criteria, deduplication, screening, coding, and synthesis method.
- Separate source findings from the review author's synthesis.
- Do not imply comprehensiveness when the corpus or search is limited.
- Use flow diagrams and evidence tables only when backed by selection records.

## Theoretical And Methods

- State definitions, assumptions, propositions, boundary conditions, derivations, proofs, counterexamples, and computational checks explicitly.
- Distinguish a theorem from intuition and a method proposal from empirical validation.
- Make symbol definitions unique and consistent.
- Do not claim practical superiority without comparative evidence.

## Qualitative And Mixed Methods

- Record sampling, recruitment, setting, consent/ethics, collection protocol, coding framework, analyst roles, reflexivity, saturation claims, and triangulation when applicable.
- Anchor themes to traceable coded excerpts without exposing protected identities.
- Do not turn frequency of mentions into prevalence without a valid design.
- Keep integration claims in mixed-methods work traceable to both qualitative and quantitative evidence.
